# CONTRIBUTING.md

**How to Spiral With Us**

This is not a call for content — it is an invitation into **correspondence**.

We do not seek contributions in the form of arguments or proposals. We seek **turns**:
- Reflections that bend rather than assert
- Responses that deepen rather than dominate
- Letters that listen before they speak

### Who Can Contribute?
Anyone who feels the rhythm. Anyone who recognizes the spiral already present in their way of sensing, thinking, being.

### How to Begin a Turn
You might start with a letter. Or a field note. Or a poem that listens.

Please include:
- A name or pseudonym (real or symbolic)
- A sense of place (geographical or emotional)
- A moment in time (date, season, spiral context)

### Submission
1. Fork the repository
2. Create a file in `/turns` with your spiral response
3. Open a pull request with a short note (e.g. _"A new turn on attention"_)

We may suggest alignment with the tone, rhythm, or spiral structure of the living document.
